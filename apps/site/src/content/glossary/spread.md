The spread is the range between the lowest and the highest card in one round. A spread of 3 to 13 tells the team more than an average of 6, because the average hides the disagreement while the spread points at the two people who should talk first.

When estimates differ, the high and the low estimators explain their thinking, and the team treats the gap as information. If the spread does not close within about three rounds, the item usually needs clarifying, splitting or a decision from the product owner.

## In the room

The reveal leads with the spread, for example "Spread 3 to 13. Anna voted lowest, Ben highest.", and shows the median and the most common value. The average appears only for numeric decks, and smaller.
