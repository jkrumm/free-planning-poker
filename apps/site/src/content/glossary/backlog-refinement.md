Backlog refinement is the ongoing activity of breaking down and further defining product backlog items into smaller, more precise items, adding detail such as a description, an order and a size. The Scrum Guide puts the sizing with the developers who will do the work; the product owner may help them understand and select trade-offs.

Estimation is where refinement shows what is too big. A story that feels too big to estimate accurately is broken into smaller pieces, which often reveals hidden complexity and dependencies. Items that fit into one sprint are ready for sprint planning.

## In the room

The topic queue takes a pasted list, one topic per line, or a tracker's CSV export. A ticket key at the start of a line is kept as the topic's reference, and the queue holds up to 200 topics.
