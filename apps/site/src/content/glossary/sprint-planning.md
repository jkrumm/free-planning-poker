Sprint planning is the Scrum event that starts a sprint by laying out the work to be performed in it. The whole Scrum team plans together: the product owner brings the most important backlog items, the developers select what they can finish, and then plan the work to turn each item into an increment.

The more the developers know about their past performance, their upcoming capacity and their definition of done, the more confident their forecast. The Scrum Guide limits the event to eight hours for a one-month sprint, and less for shorter sprints.
