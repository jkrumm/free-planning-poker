Planning poker is a consensus-based technique in which each team member picks a numbered card in private and all cards are revealed at the same time. If the cards differ, the people with the lowest and the highest card explain their reasoning and the team votes again. James Grenning defined and named it in 2002, and Mike Cohn popularised it. Teams most often play it with story points, but any estimating unit works.

## In the room

A vote stays on the server until the reveal; before it, the other seats see only that you have voted. Any player can reveal, and spectators watch without blocking a reveal.
