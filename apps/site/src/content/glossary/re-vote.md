A re-vote is a second round of planning poker on the same item, played after the team has discussed the first reveal. The high and low estimators explain their reasoning, the team shares what it learned, and everyone chooses again.

One to three rounds are usually enough. An item that still splits the team after that often needs clarification, splitting or a decision, not a fourth round.

## In the room

A re-vote keeps the topic and clears the cards. The other ways to leave a revealed round are to settle a final value or to move on.
