Consensus in planning poker means the team has enough agreement to use the estimate, not that every card matches. The team does not need perfect agreement; it needs enough for the decision the estimate will serve.

When rounds keep ending apart, the team can ask whether everyone can support one particular estimate. That differs from averaging: supporting a number means the concerns have been heard, the assumptions are understood and the team can live with it for this decision.

## In the room

When every card is the same, that value becomes the topic's final value at once and the room celebrates. The summary line names the outcome as consensus, near consensus (only neighbouring cards) or split.
