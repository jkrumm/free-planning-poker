T-shirt sizing is relative estimation with the numbers taken off: the team labels each item XS, S, M, L or XL, judged against the other items in front of it. It suits an early, rough pass over a backlog nobody has sized yet, to find the items that are clearly too big and need splitting.

Sizes do not add up, since an S plus an M is not an L, so they cannot feed a velocity. For stories that are about to enter a sprint, re-estimate with numbers. A common mapping to start from is XS to 1, S to 2, M to 3 or 5, L to 8 and XL to 13 or more, but it is a starting band, not a formula.

## In the room

T-shirt is a deck. Its sizes map onto the Fibonacci numbers, so history and statistics compare across decks, and the distance between cards is measured in deck positions, so the spread works for sizes as it does for numbers.
