Relative estimation sizes a piece of work by comparing it with other pieces of work, not by predicting how many hours it takes. Story points and T-shirt sizes are both relative: an M is an M because it is clearly bigger than the S and clearly smaller than the L next to it.

To make the comparison stable, pick a few reference items the whole team knows, for example a simple 1-point story, a moderate 5-point story and a complex 13-point story, and size new work against them.
