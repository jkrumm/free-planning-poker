Story points are a unit that expresses the relative effort of a user story, weighing complexity, risk and the amount of work instead of hours or days. A story of 5 points is bigger than one of 3 and smaller than one of 8; the number says nothing about how long it takes.

Every team estimates on its own scale, so one team's 5 is not another team's 5. That is why points are not compared across teams, and why a team's velocity only means something to that team.

## In the room

Every card carries a number behind its label, so results and statistics compare across decks.
