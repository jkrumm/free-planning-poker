The Fibonacci sequence is the series 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21 in which each number is the sum of the two before it, and it is the typical scale of planning poker cards. The gaps widen as the numbers grow, which mirrors how uncertainty grows with the size of a task: the team picks between 5 and 8 instead of arguing about 6 against 7.

A deck that doubled each value would claim a precision nobody has. A task about twice a 5 is either a bit less than double (8) or a bit more (13), and the sequence forces that choice. Some commercial decks stop following the sequence after 13 and use 20, 40 and 100.

## In the room

Fibonacci is the default deck. Modified Fibonacci, T-shirt, powers of two and custom decks are available, each with a ? card to abstain and a break card.
