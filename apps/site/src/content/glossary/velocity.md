Velocity is the amount of work a team completes in one sprint, usually counted in story points. The average over several sprints is the number a team plans against: 60 points over 3 sprints is a velocity of 20.

It describes what happened; it is not a target. Tied to rewards or reviews, it invites inflated estimates, and comparing the velocity of two teams says nothing because each team estimates on its own scale. A stable velocity is a better sign than a rising one.
