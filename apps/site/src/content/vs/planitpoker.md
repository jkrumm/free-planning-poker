## Where PlanITpoker is better

It claims a community of 90,000 developers, by its own count. We state no such number. It takes stories from a Jira XML export as well as CSV, where we take a pasted list or a CSV. Its paid plan removes the participant limit entirely, while our room stops at 30.

## Where Free Planning Poker is better

A free room holds more people. PlanITpoker's free plan is for 7 participants, and every extra seat is a $20 a month decision. Here 30 players join for free, and there is no paid plan.

## Which one to pick

Pick PlanITpoker if your team is under 7, you import from Jira XML, or you need a room with no player limit and will pay for it. Pick Free Planning Poker for 8 to 30 players at no cost.
