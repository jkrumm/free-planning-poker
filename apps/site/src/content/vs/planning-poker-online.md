## Where Planning Poker Online is better

It reaches your tracker. Linear is a native integration, and a Jira plugin sits on the Atlassian Marketplace. Free Planning Poker has no tracker integration at launch: you paste a list or drop a CSV export, and copy the result out. Its free plan has no player limit, while our room seats 30. It shows ISO 27001 and GDPR badges, and we hold no certificate.

## Where Free Planning Poker is better

Nothing runs out. Planning Poker Online's free plan stops at 9 voting rounds and 5 voted issues per game, and a long backlog passes both quickly. There is no cap on rounds here, and no paid plan to move to. History is a Premium feature there; here every round is kept.

## Which one to pick

Pick Planning Poker Online if your estimates must land in Linear or Jira through an integration, or if you need a room above 30 players. Pick Free Planning Poker if you want to run a long backlog in one room and pay nothing.
