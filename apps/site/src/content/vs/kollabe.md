## Where Kollabe is better

It is wired into your tracker. Paid plans import issues from Jira, GitHub, Azure DevOps and Linear and sync the estimates back, and Premium adds a public API and an MCP server. We have none of that at launch. Kollabe also says one room can take up to 500 users; ours stops at 30. Its pages give 10 participants for the free plan and 500 for the large team feature, and do not say which plan has the large team feature, so ask before you plan a big room around it.

## Where Free Planning Poker is better

The free plan has more room. Kollabe's free plan stops at 10 players and 4 planning poker meetings a month, with 7 days of history. Here a room seats 30, the number of sessions is not limited, and history stays.

## Which one to pick

Pick Kollabe if estimates have to flow into Jira, GitHub, Azure DevOps or Linear, and a paid plan is acceptable. Pick Free Planning Poker for a free team of up to 30 that estimates every week and keeps its history.
