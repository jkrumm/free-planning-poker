## Where Parabol is better

It is a suite. Sprint Poker pulls stories from Jira, GitHub and GitLab on the free plan and writes the estimate back to the issue. It adds a second dimension to a scale, so a team can score value and effort for WSJF. It runs retrospectives and health checks too. Free Planning Poker does estimation only, and none of this at launch.

## Where Free Planning Poker is better

It is one room and nothing else. Parabol's free plan stops at 2 teams, 10 meetings a month and 30 days of history, and the next step is $8 per active user per month. Here there is no plan to outgrow. Parabol's licence is Business Source 1.1, which its own file says is not an open source licence until each version converts to AGPL-3.0 four years on; ours is AGPL-3.0 now. Parabol's pricing and Sprint Poker pages do not say whether a player can join without an account. Free Planning Poker has no account.

## Which one to pick

Pick Parabol if the estimates must flow into Jira, GitHub or GitLab, or the team wants retrospectives in the same tool. Pick Free Planning Poker for fast estimation with no setup and nothing to count.
