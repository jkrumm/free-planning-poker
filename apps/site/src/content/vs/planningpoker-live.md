## Where PlanningPoker.live is better

It meets the team where it works. It runs inside Zoom, Teams, Meet and Webex, imports from Jira and Linear and pushes the estimates back, and takes Slack commands. We have none of those at launch. Anyone can join a room with no limit on the number of voters; our room stops at 30. It also lists security reviews by Microsoft, Google, Zoom and Cisco Webex, and we have none to name.

## Where Free Planning Poker is better

Opening a room costs nothing. On PlanningPoker.live every new room costs a credit: five to start, one more a month after registering, and $9 to $50 for more. The starter credits come with ads. Here a room is free, as many as you want, with no credits and no ads.

## Which one to pick

Pick PlanningPoker.live if you estimate inside a video call or write estimates back to Jira or Linear, and you open few rooms. Pick Free Planning Poker if you open a room for every refinement and do not want to count them.
